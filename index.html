My Word

Give your word. Give it your best. Keep going.

My Word is a goal and accountability app for adults. You set a Challenge, track Wins and Slip-ups as you go, and optionally add a private Pledge.

Privacy Policy Delete your account

How it works

Set a Challenge

Describe what you will do, when it starts and ends, and how often. Challenges can be for yourself, shared with people you invite, or Joint.

Track Wins and Slip-ups

Log a Win when you keep your word. Log a Slip-up when you miss. Progress is self-reported — My Word does not read health apps or location.

Optional Pledge

You may set a private Pledge amount. It is optional and never a debt. Card payments, when used, are processed by Stripe. Pledge amounts are not shared with followers.

What My Word is — and is not

  • My Word is a goal-tracking and accountability product.
  • It is not medical, fitness, nutritional, psychological, or financial advice.
  • It is for people aged 18 and over.
